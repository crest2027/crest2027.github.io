CREST 2027

Workshop on Formal Reasoning about Causation, Responsibility, and Explanations in Science and Technology

To be held as part of CAV 2027

July 20, 2027 Amsterdam, the Netherlands

Call for Presentations

CREST brings together researchers from the different communities that study causality, responsibility, and explanations. The workshop is a place to exchange ideas across these communities and to present and discuss recent results and new directions.

Topics of interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Languages and logics for causal specification and causal analysis
  • Derivation of causal models (e.g., during runtime verification / observation)
  • Actual causality in hybrid, cyber-physical and ML-based systems
  • Causality and agency attribution in AI systems
  • Causality in socio-technical and legal systems
  • Causal reasoning for security engineering
  • Causality in accident analysis, safety cases, and certification
  • Fault ascription and blaming
  • Accountability and explainability of algorithms and systems
  • Causal model inference and cause mining
  • Applications, implementations, tools and case studies of the above

Submission guidelines

We invite presentation proposals in the form of an extended abstract of up to 3 pages (excluding references) in LNCS format.

Work that overlaps with previously published results is welcome. Proposals will be selected based on their relevance to the workshop topics, and reviewing is single-blind.

Submission instructions will be announced soon.
